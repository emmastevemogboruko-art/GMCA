Gospel Music Community Africa is grateful for the people and organizations who have chosen to stand with us.

Through mentorship, financial partnership, music production, project collaboration and artistic contribution, these individuals and organizations are helping create opportunities for gospel music ministers, worship leaders, choirs and creatives to grow and serve with excellence.
