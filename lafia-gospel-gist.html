LAFIA GOSPEL GIST

Everyone Deserves a Platform

All around us are gifted individuals, talented, authentic, and full of God-given potential looking for expression.

Not because they lack value or ability, but because they lack opportunity and the right platform.

Visibility is not always determined by ability but by opportunities. The people we celebrate today did not simply appear overnight. They were given opportunities, platforms, and moments that allowed their gifts to be seen and appreciated.

We believe everyone deserves that opportunity. Everyone deserves a platform. Everyone deserves the chance to express what God has deposited within them — including you.

OUR MOVEMENT

More Than an Organization. A Movement.

LAFIA GOSPEL GIST is more than an organization, it is a movement with a clear mission: to discover you, develop you, refine you and promote you and other young, passionate, and gifted individuals who are ready to make a lasting impact for the Kingdom of God and society.

At Lafia Gospel Gist, we are committed to identifying and nurturing raw potential.

Our mission is to help you move from hidden potential to visible impact, providing opportunities for growth, exposure, collaboration, mentorship, and meaningful influence.

ONE STAGE
DIFFERENT TALENT

One Stage Different Talent Show is an annual Gospel talent discovery, development, showcase and awards platform hosted by Lafia Gospel Gist Talent Hub, created to discover, celebrate, develop and reward exceptional talents among young people.

We believe that every gift carries a voice and every talent deserves an opportunity to shine.

Through this platform, young people from churches, fellowships and communities are given an opportunity to showcase their gifts, gain visibility, connect with opportunities, learn from others, and receive recognition for outstanding performances.
