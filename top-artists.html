ABOUT THE RECOGNITION

Celebrating Excellence in Gospel Music Ministry

The GMCA Top 10 Outstanding Gospel Artists Recognition celebrates gospel music ministers whose dedication, excellence and Kingdom impact continue to inspire lives through music. This inaugural edition honours outstanding participants of the Lafia–Akwanga Mega Gospel Music Production, Promotion & Marketing Project.
